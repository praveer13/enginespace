# Questions in a lesson (the item block)

## Scope

- **Surface:** the `item` block of a lesson (A§4.3), drawn by `src/items/ItemBlock.tsx`: a lesson's Test and the checkpoints in Read, in Operate mode.
- **Visitor:** a learner partway through a lesson, who has just read the idea and now answers on numbers they have not seen.
- **Action:** start the questions, write one answer, say how sure they are if they like, check it, read why, and ask for new numbers.
- **Proof:** the numbers are drawn when the attempt starts and the key is computed from them on submit and stored nowhere; every figure carries its tag, and a synthetic world says so.
- **Constraints:** the PRODUCT.md constraints; 360-390 px and keyboard alone; nothing timed; the confidence choice never gates; every duration 0 under reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A question is a sheet of the course's own paper with the learner's own numbers on it: the givens printed with their tags, one answer field with its unit, and why printed under it once the answer is checked. It refuses the quiz card of four radio rows and a green or red banner.

OWN-WORLD: Data Book (REDESIGN §2.8): flat paper cut square with one soft sheet shadow and no texture; ink text in Atkinson Hyperlegible, titles in Big Shoulders Display 800 in sentence case; every number the learner compares in the tabular mono face with its tag; one amber marker that means "now", only on the control to act on now, a diagram's now cursor and the current position in a rail or on the course track; the code colours only where the model's state is drawn.

STORY: The learner sees that the numbers were drawn the moment they started, so the answer cannot be looked up, and that "guess" is an honest thing to say. They read why in their own numbers and want new ones. The pass rule is stated once, above the questions it governs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inside a lesson's Test: the pass rule's sentence, then the sheet: the question in the engine's words, the givens, the answer field, "How sure are you?" with guess, think so and sure, and Check answer. Before a set starts, Start questions is the amber control where it is the move in hand; after a passing set the lesson's way on is amber and New numbers an outline.

FORM: Data Book's question sheet (REDESIGN §2.5). seed: none (a specified surface inside the established world, which the owner confirmed at the look gate; no roll)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this surface ends with wave R's finish review (R-T50), its material fixes, and DESIGN.md written from the built world

## Memorable moment

The first wrong answer read back in the learner's own numbers, with the step where it went wrong named, and New numbers beside it.
